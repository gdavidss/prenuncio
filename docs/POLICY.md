### 1.7 What changed in 2025 and 2026

- Fire: record burning in 2024 under drought. A 2026 Nature paper (10.1038/s41586-026-11168-1) reports 38% of the clearing PRODES mapped in 2024 as following fire [S].
- Congress: licensing law 15.190/2025, with 52 of 63 vetoes overturned on 27 Nov 2025, in force 4 Feb 2026, including a licence waiver for farming on properties with a pending CAR [S]. PL 2.564/2025, which restricts embargoes based on remote sensing alone and may bar destruction of equipment, passed the Chamber on 20 May 2026 and has been in Senate committees since Aug 2026 [S]. IBAMA says up to 70% of its Amazon actions depend on these measures.
- Budget: the 2026 budget cut IBAMA by 17.2% and ICMBio by 22.9% (figures cited at the STF) [V, STF page]; a provisional measure of 15 Jun 2026 added R$ 337.5 million for fire and enforcement [S].
- Credit: the PRODES rule was postponed twice (above).
- Politics: Lula re-elected on 25 Oct 2026 [S]. The dollar moved from 5.04 to 5.50 the next trading day and stood at 5.65 on 13 Nov [V, BCB SGS series 1].
- Climate: ONI at +2.46 for Aug to Oct 2026 [V, NOAA file]. A strong El Niño is under way.

Two readings matter for the panel. First, a regression of clearing on notices with fixed effects is biased toward zero by reverse causality, which fits the 4% to 9% found; the published elasticities come from an instrument. Second, the Journal of Politics result is direct evidence for an expectations channel that enforcement counts do not carry, which fits a fall common to all municipalities.

## 4. Confounders and speed of response

- Exchange rate and prices. The World Bank model has a 1% real appreciation lowering clearing 1.13% the following year, and the real appreciated about 17% from May 2021 to May 2023. Assunção, Gandour and Rocha (2015, 10.1017/S1355770X15000078) attribute about half of the 2005 to 2009 fall to policy after controlling for prices. Cattle prices fell through 2023 and recovered from late 2024 [M], so prices helped early and stopped helping later, while clearing kept falling.
- El Niño and fire. Drought in 2023 to 2024 raised fire and degradation while clear-cutting fell. Burned forest is later mapped as clearing, which lifts PRODES 2024 and 2025 with no change in intent. Track DETER clear-cut classes apart from fire-scar and degradation classes. A strong El Niño is under way now.
- The 2022 surge. DETER for Aug to Dec 2022 was a record, and PRODES 2023 contains those five months. The government's split is +54% for Aug to Dec 2022 and -42% for Jan to Jul 2023 [M]. Part of the 2023 fall is the end of anticipatory clearing. Election-cycle evidence: Pailler 2018, JEEM, 10.1016/j.jeem.2018.01.008 (8% to 10% more clearing in municipal election years). No paper on the 2022 surge was found.
- Cloud. DETER misses clearing under cloud and catches up later, which moves alerts between months. The state-by-month cloud file above is the public control. PRODES polygons carry `def_cloud`.
- Composition. The fall is concentrated in undesignated public forest and settlements in Pará and Amazonas (IPAM), with relocation to remote areas.

Speed. Every causal estimate uses yearly data with enforcement in t-1. Nothing sub-annual has been estimated. Three facts point to a within-year component: alerts fell 42% within seven months of the change of government [M], signals moved clearing in 2019 to 2022 without any change in sanctions, and the 2024 strike is reported to have cut notices by about 70% for seven months without a visible rebound in clearing. That last figure is from the union and the press and does not match a notices-per-alert ratio that stayed between 85 and 170, so check it in the notices file before using the strike as a shock. Deterrence reads as a stock of perceived risk, not a monthly flow. Test distributed lags of 0 to 12 months and expect the long lags to carry the weight.

Designs that have survived review, and where 2023+ offers the same leverage:
- Cloud cover as an instrument for DETER-driven enforcement (AEJ Applied). Public cloud is state by month; a municipal instrument needs to be built from imagery.
- Difference-in-differences around list entry (EDE, REStud). The 9 Nov 2023 and 11 Nov 2024 lists, with the rule in Portaria 833, allow the same design and a threshold comparison. Expect spillovers to neighbours.
- Biome-border comparison for credit (Economic Journal). For 2023+, use exposure: the pre-2023 share of a municipality's financed area inside undesignated public forest, Indigenous land, conservation units or embargoes, interacted with 1 Aug 2023, 2 Jan 2024 and 2 Jan 2025. The six weeks from 1 Apr to 12 May 2026 when the PRODES block was live above 4 fiscal modules, and the 4 and 15 module thresholds, are a clean window.
- Border discontinuities for Indigenous lands (PNAS) fit the removal operations as event studies.
- Remote embargo batches P1 to P11 are dated and target PRODES polygons in priority municipalities. Compare embargoed polygons and their surroundings with matched polygons not yet reached.
- The 2024 strike is a dated negative shock to field presence.

## 5. Five series to add first

1. Remote embargo intensity (IBAMA embargo file, tag "CONTROLE REMOTO"). Hectares newly embargoed by remote terms in the trailing 12 months divided by DETER alert area in the same window, by municipality and month. Add the active stock: embargoed hectares not yet lifted (embargo history file) divided by accumulated PRODES clearing since 2008. Keep collective IN 15 embargoes as a separate dummy.
2. Credit exposure to the CMN rules (SICOR municipality file plus plot and CAR tables). Real value of cattle working-capital and investment credit per month relative to the same month's 2019 to 2022 mean, and a fixed exposure share (2019 to 2022 financed area on properties overlapping undesignated public forest, Indigenous land, conservation units, embargoes, or with CAR now suspended or cancelled) interacted with the three effective dates.
3. Priority list and programme status (hand-coded from Portarias 251/2022, 834/2023, 1.202/2024 and the adhesion records). Listed dummy, months since listing, months since adhesion to União com Municípios, and share of neighbours listed.
4. Asset loss (IBAMA destruction and seizure files). Destruction terms in the trailing 6 months per 100 km2 of alerts, split into clearing equipment and mining equipment by DES_TIPO_BEM, plus cattle seizures where the goods table allows.
5. National enforcement capacity (Portal da Transparência). Paid amount under 214N plus 168N plus 214P, 12-month sum in real terms, with a dummy for 2 Jan to 12 Aug 2024. It is collinear with time effects and identifies nothing causally, but it is the only dated carrier of the common component for a forecast.
