# Prenúncio brand guidelines

Prenúncio is the sign before the event: an early warning for the Brazilian Amazon. It says where forest will be cleared, burned, degraded or mined next, and how hard the pressure is now. It is an alarm, not a magazine. It looks like warning signage and reads like an emergency bulletin.

## Voice

Number first, then the fact, then stop. One short sentence per idea, present tense, no adjectives, no reassurance, no exclamation marks. Measured and forecast are never dressed as each other, and a quantity that was not tested is not shown. English and Portuguese carry equal weight. No em dashes. In Portuguese, no label followed by a colon.

## Colour

| Name | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Signal red | `#e4002b` | The alarm and nothing else. How much of it is on screen is the alert level. |
| Ink | `#111111` | Type, rules, measured history. |
| Ground | `#f2f2ef` | The sign's white field. |
| Grey | `#5c5c58` | Secondary type. |
| Clearing violet | `#5b2bbf` | Clearing risk. |
| Fire amber | `#f2a900` | Fire risk: yellow amber, kept well away from red. |
| Degradation teal | `#0b7c86` | Degradation and selective logging. |
| Mining graphite | `#2b2b2b` | Mining. |
| Forest, sand, water | `#acc7a6` `#e8e1d0` `#a8c6d6` | The base map. Quiet on purpose. |

Red and the hazards cannot collide because they live in different places. Red is the level and never fills the map. Each hazard owns a hue outside red and a glyph (notched square, flame, bars, diamond), so it is told apart by shape as well as colour. Clearing is violet so it can never read as forest on fire.

## Type

Barlow Condensed and Barlow, one family drawn from highway signs and number plates. Condensed at weight 800 sets the wordmark, the level and the headline, because a warning is read from a distance. Regular width sets everything small, with tabular figures. Capitals are for the wordmark, the level and controls.

## How urgency scales

The level is counted, never chosen. For the hazard and place on screen:

- its own alerts in the last three months against the same months a year before: 10% up is 1 point, double is 2;
- for the whole Amazon, a strong El Niño (ONI of 1.5 or more) is 1 point;
- for a sector, the top 10% of its kind for forecast risk (or the top three) is 1 point, the top 2% (or first place) is 2.

| Points | Level | Red on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Quiet | None. Even the mark is ink. |
| 1 | Watch | The mark, the level word, one bar of the meter. |
| 2 | Warning | A full red band across the column. |
| 3 or more | Alarm | The whole bulletin turns red. |

On the timeline, red marks only what is above last year in the three months that set the level. In the signals, red marks only what is rising for the place on screen. A replayed past year has no level: the alarm is about now.

## Never

- Red as decoration, as a map fill, or on anything that is not worse than a year ago.
- A level the data does not earn, in either direction.
- Fire in red, or clearing in a flame colour.
- Boxes that hold one number, accent bars beside text, footers, tables in the interface.
- Paragraphs on the first screen. Depth is one click away.
